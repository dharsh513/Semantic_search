Search the literature
by meaning, not keywords.

Ask in plain English

Your question is expanded with NCBI's MeSH mapping — no Boolean operators, no wildcards, no truncation rules to memorise.

Ranked by semantics

Abstracts are embedded locally and re-ranked with a hybrid semantic + BM25 scorer, so vocabulary mismatches stop hiding the paper you needed.

Answers that cite

Every sentence of an answer is lifted verbatim from a retrieved abstract and tagged with its PMID. Nothing is invented.

Chat with any paper

Open a result and ask it questions. It quotes the abstract back with a confidence score — and says so when it doesn't know.

Export in one click

Vancouver/NLM citations as PDF, BibTeX or RIS — a reference list or a full report with the answer and every abstract.

Data from NCBI PubMed via E-utilities · no API key required · embeddings run on your machine
